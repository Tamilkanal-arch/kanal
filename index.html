📖 Leave Record — User Guide & Business Rules

This application is the converted system for the Kuwait office leave management workbook (LEAVE_RECORD.xlsx). All calculations are live and automatic.

1. Calendar Sheet Data Entry

  • Employee Name is required (Employee ID and Designation are optional).
  • Click on any date to mark a leave type, or focus and press:
P Paid Leave M Medical Leave U Unpaid Leave O Other Leave

Leave blank for a normal working day.

Kuwait Company Holiday (Friday Rule):
Friday is the company holiday. Leave codes marked on a Friday are visually shaded and are strictly excluded from all official leave totals in Master Sheet 1.

2. Leave Register Sheet Data Entry

  • Columns A-D: Employee ID, Employee Name, Designation, and Joining Date.
  • Monthly Breakdown (Jan - Dec): Enter the number of days taken for Paid, Medical, Other, and Unpaid leave each month.
  • Annual Paid Leave Entitlement: Enter the total allocated paid leave days per year (e.g. 30, 45).
  • All remaining totals, balances, and accrued figures calculate automatically.

3. Accrued Leave & Balance vs Accrued Formula

Leave accrues monthly at a constant rate starting from the employee's Joining Date and accumulates indefinitely across years (never reset each January):

months_elapsed = (current_year - joining_year) * 12 + (current_month - joining_month) + 1 Accrued_Leave_Till_Date = max(0, (Annual_Paid_Entitlement / 12) * months_elapsed) Balance_vs_Accrued = Accrued_Leave_Till_Date - Total_Paid_Taken

4. Master Sheets & Admin Protection

Master Sheet (Calendar Summary) and Master Sheet 2 (Balances & Accruals) are read-only summary views to preserve data integrity. To unlock administrator controls or edit sensitive configurations, click the Read-Only Views button in the top header and enter the authorized administrator password provided by management.
